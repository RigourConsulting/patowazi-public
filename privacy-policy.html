4. On-Device AI & Silent Helper Intelligence

PatoWazi includes two on-device intelligence subsystems:

  1. On-Device Receipt OCR Engine: Recognizes text from photographed receipts and PDFs directly on your phone using local optical character recognition and deterministic financial parsing. No images or text are uploaded to cloud vision APIs.
  2. Silent Helper Intelligence Engine: Computes counterparty suggestions, median transaction amounts, shop catalogue price matches, and duplicate/extra-zero warnings directly from your local SQLite database. No external LLM or cloud AI service is ever contacted.

5. Data Storage, Portable Backups & User-Initiated Sharing

  • Local Sandbox Storage: All active records reside in your device's private application storage, protected by Android and iOS operating-system sandboxing and full-disk encryption.
  • Encrypted Portable Backups: When you export a backup archive, PatoWazi encrypts your ledger and document vault using authenticated AES-256-GCM encryption with a 256-bit key derived from your passphrase via PBKDF2WithHmacSHA256 (210,000 iterations) and a cryptographic SHA-256 manifest integrity check.
  • User-Controlled Sharing: When you tap Share on an invoice PDF, WhatsApp receipt card, CSV export, or backup archive, the file is handed to your chosen app via the OS share sheet under your sole control.

6. Regional & International Privacy Rights

Because all data is stored locally on your device and never transmitted to Rigour Consulting, you exercise direct, immediate technical control over your personal and business data at all times:

  • East African Data Protection Laws: Compliant by design with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 (ODPC), the Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 (PDPO), and the Tanzania Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 (PDPC) Principles of Data Minimization, Purpose Limitation, and Storage Security.
  • GDPR (EEA/UK) & CCPA/CPRA (California): You have the right to access, export (via CSV/PDF/Backup), rectify (via ledger corrections/reversals), and erase (via local app storage clearing) 100% of your data directly on your device. Rigour Consulting does not sell or share personal information as defined under CCPA/CPRA.
  • Children's Privacy (COPPA / Apple Kids Category / Google Play Families): PatoWazi is a commercial bookkeeping utility intended for business owners and adults (ages 18+). It is not directed at children under 13 and never collects personal information from children.
