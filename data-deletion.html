1. Zero Cloud Accounts — Nothing Stored on Developer Servers

PatoWazi does not require or offer cloud account registration, and Rigour Consulting never collects or stores copies of your data on any remote server. Consequently, there is no server-side account or cloud profile held by Rigour Consulting to delete.

2. Step-by-Step Guide to Deleting Data on Your Device

You have full, instant control to delete individual records or wipe all PatoWazi data from your device at any time without contacting support:

What You Want to Delete Exact Steps Inside PatoWazi / Device Settings Immediate Effect
Single Calendar Reminder / Task Open Calendar, tap the date (DD-MM-YYYY), locate the task, and tap Delete. Cancels the task and removes scheduled local notifications.
Shop Catalogue Item or Quick Template Open Hub → Trader Tools, locate the item or template, and tap Delete. Immediately removes the item/template from local storage.
Scanned Document / Receipt Image Open Hub → Documents & OCR and tap Archive on the document. Removes the document from active views.
Complete Erase of All Business & Personal Records On Android: Open Settings → Apps → PatoWazi → Storage → Clear storage / Clear data (or uninstall PatoWazi).
On iOS: Open Settings → General → iPhone Storage → PatoWazi → Delete App. 		Permanently destroys the local SQLite database, all Document Vault files, OCR drafts, and the PBKDF2 PIN hash on the device.
Exported Backup / CSV / PDF Files Open your device's Files / Downloads app and delete any .pwbackup, .csv, or .pdf files you previously exported. Permanently removes exported files from your chosen folder.
