2. Frequently Asked Questions (Maswali Yanayoulizwa Mara kwa Mara)

Q1: How do I back up my business records or move to a new phone?

Open Hub → Backup, Restore & CSV Export. Enter a strong passphrase (at least 8 characters) and tap Export Encrypted Backup. Save the generated archive to a safe folder or cloud drive. On your new phone, install PatoWazi, open Hub → Backup & Restore, select the backup file, and enter the same passphrase to restore your entire business ledger, personal loans, and document vault.

Q2: Why are dates formatted as DD-MM-YYYY?

PatoWazi uses the standard East African day-first date format (DD-MM-YYYY, for example 19-10-2026) across all screens, receipts, invoices, and calendar reminders. When typing a date manually, PatoWazi also accepts 19/10/2026, 19.10.2026, 19 Oct 2026, or 2026-10-19.

Q3: How do I get the sharpest OCR scan on faded thermal receipts?

Place the paper receipt on a flat surface in good lighting and tap Camera inside Hub → Documents & OCR. After capture, tap Review OCR. If the thermal ink is faded or shadowed, tap one of the enhancement buttons—Faded Thermal, Shadow Binarize, or Rotate 90°—to re-enhance and re-scan the receipt offline. You can also copy an M-Pesa confirmation SMS and tap Paste M-Pesa / Receipt Text.

Q4: What if I made a mistake in a saved sale, purchase, or payment?

To protect audit integrity, PatoWazi uses an append-only ledger. Open Records, find the transaction, and tap Reverse (or Correct on a debt payment). PatoWazi will record an explicit reversal entry and let you post the corrected figure transparently.

Q5: What happens if I forget my App Lock PIN?

Because PatoWazi has no cloud server and stores only a one-way cryptographic hash (PBKDF2WithHmacSHA256) of your PIN on your phone, we cannot remotely reset your PIN. If you have an encrypted backup archive and remember your backup passphrase, you can clear app data in system settings and restore your encrypted backup.
